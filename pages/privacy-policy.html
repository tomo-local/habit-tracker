Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-26

habit-tracker ("the app") is an open-source command-line tool that helps you record daily habits as events on your own Google Calendar. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

The app uses the Google Calendar API with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope to:

  • List your existing calendars, so you can choose one to use
  • Create a dedicated calendar to store habit records, if you choose to
  • Create and read events on that calendar to record and display your habits

The app does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or any other Google data, and does not read or modify calendars other than the one you select for tracking.

How your data is stored and used

habit-tracker runs entirely on your own computer. It is not a hosted service: there is no backend server operated by the developer, and no data is sent to the developer or any third party.

Your OAuth token and local configuration (selected calendar, tracked habits) are stored only on your machine, by default under ~/.config/habit/. All communication with Google Calendar happens directly between your machine and Google's servers.

Data sharing

The app does not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third party. habit-tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke habit-tracker's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or by running habit-tracker auth remove. Deleting the ~/.config/habit/ directory removes all local data created by the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Changes will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be filed as an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
